Grade 8 Semester 1 Grammar Guide

Gerunds vs. Infinitives

• Verb + Infinitive: want, decide, refuse, hope (e.g., refused to turn)

• Verb + Gerund: keep, stop, avoid, mind (e.g., kept talking)

• Sense Verbs: saw/heard + object + bare infinitive (entire action) OR gerund (action in progress).

• Infinitive of Purpose: to receive, to buy (explains why an action is done).

Phrasal Verbs & Particles

Particles alter verb direction and flow: up (increase/upward/complete), down (decrease/downward), on (continue), off (leave/depart), over (across surface/avoid/transfer), out (extend).

Quantifiers as Pronouns

Quantifiers (some, any, many, a few, several, none) stand alone as pronouns when the context noun is clear: "I saw shoes. I'd like some too."

Adverbs & Persuasive Adjectives

• Adverbs modify adjectives/verbs: highly effective, post directly.

• Informative vs. Persuasive Adjectives: Informative = facts (portable, digital); Persuasive = appealing (breathtaking, stunning).

• Direct Address & Imperatives: Engaging the reader using second-person pronouns ("your") and command verbs ("Unleash").

As vs. Like

• Like: Preposition followed by a noun/pronoun (moves like a hawk).

• As: Indicates a function/role (used a stick as a lever) or functions as a conjunction (as the tide retreated).

Punctuation & Sentence Structure

• Commas vs. Dashes: Commas separate clauses; dashes (—) add emphasis, sudden pauses, or highlight key details.

• Incomplete/Minor Sentences: Lack a standard subject+verb for dramatic effect or emotional impact (e.g., "Bright, bright red.").
