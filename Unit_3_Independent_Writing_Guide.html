What Makes a Good Adventure Story?
Checklist from your Cambridge Learner's Book:

1. First Person

Use "I" or "We". It must look like it happened directly to you!

2. A Complication

A chain of events where things suddenly go dangerous or unexpected.

3. Inner Thoughts

Describe exactly what you were thinking and feeling during the crisis.

4. Strong Contrasts

Contrast ordinary settings with sudden chaos using vivid sensory words.
