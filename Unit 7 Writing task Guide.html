Step 2 of 6

Character & Voice Contrast Planner

In Cambridge Unit 7, characters often represent **symbolic concepts** (e.g., Greed, Pollution, Future Child, Forest Keeper). Establish distinct voice styles!

Cambridge Stylistic Contrast Rule:

Give Antagonists/Villains short, forceful exclamations ("Move!", "Silence!", "Money talks!") and give Protective Figures extended, reflective conditional arguments ("If you destroy these trees, the whole ecosystem will collapse.").

Step 3 of 6

Dramatic Conventions & Layout Checklist

Play scripts follow specific structural rules. Study these key conventions before writing.

1

Speaker Names on Left

Write character names in CAPITAL LETTERS on the left side followed by a colon. Never indent or start with speech marks.

MONEY:
  Stand back! This forest belongs to me.
2

NO Quotation Marks

Unlike fiction stories, play scripts NEVER use quotation marks ("...") around spoken dialogue!

CHILD: "We will protect our land!"
CHILD: We will protect our land!
3

Stage Directions in Brackets

Describe actions, gestures, and tone of voice using brackets and italics.

SMOKE: (coughing violently, pointing towards the trees) We are moving in!
4

Scene Setup Description

Begin your scene with italicized descriptions of stage placement, backdrop lighting, and sound effects.

Lights up on stage. A dense ancient canopy covers the background. Soft birdsong fades as heavy machinery rumbles.

Cambridge Stage 9 Language Toolkit

Integrate compulsory Stage 9 grammar features: Conditional Structures & Environment Collocations.

1. Target Conditional Structures

1st Conditional

Real Cause & Effect

If + Present Simple, Will + Verb

"If you chop down these trees, the ecosystem will collapse."

2nd Conditional

Hypothetical Scenario

If + Past Simple, Would + Verb

"If you had any compassion, you would not contaminate our river."

3rd Conditional

Past Regret & Consequence

If + Past Perfect, Would Have + Past Participle

"If we had protected this forest last year, these animals wouldn't have suffered."
